City of Wadsworth: Brocade ICX to Arista Campus Switch Migration

The City of Wadsworth needed to replace an aging Brocade ICX access layer that had grown difficult to manage and support. The project involved a full access layer overhaul across multiple municipal sites, migrating to a modern Arista campus switching platform at scale, with zero unplanned downtime to city services.
