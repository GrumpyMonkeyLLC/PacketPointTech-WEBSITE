Our Story

Founded on transparency and technical honesty

Packet Point Technologies was built on a straightforward premise: businesses deserve IT infrastructure that works reliably, scales without drama, and is backed by genuine technical expertise — not sales pitches or vendor lock-in.

We bring hands-on enterprise experience across BGP-routed campus networks, hybrid Azure deployments, compliance-driven environments, and large-scale switching projects — and we apply that same depth of knowledge to every client engagement, regardless of size or budget.

Our approach has always been documentation-first. Every project ships with complete infrastructure documentation, knowledge transfer to your team, and monitoring configured before we walk out the door. You should understand your own environment — that's non-negotiable for us.

Based in Northeast Ohio, we work with municipalities, nonprofits, and growing businesses that need infrastructure and a web presence they can count on.

Our Values

What guides every engagement

The principles behind every recommendation, every architecture decision, and every line of documentation we write.

01

Technical honesty

We tell you what you need to hear, not what's easiest to sell. If a simpler solution solves the problem, that's what we recommend — even if it means less work for us.

02

Documentation as a deliverable

Every project ships with complete documentation. Network diagrams, runbooks, IP addressing, device configs — all of it, always. Your team should be able to work without us.

03

Reliability by design

Redundancy, monitoring, and failover aren't afterthoughts. They're architectural requirements we build in from the start, not features we sell you later.

04

Long-term thinking

We design for where your business is going in three years, not just where it is today. Scalability and maintainability are first-class requirements on every project.
