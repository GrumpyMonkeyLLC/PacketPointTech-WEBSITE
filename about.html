Our Story

Founded on a commitment to transparency

Packet Point Technologies was built on a straightforward premise: businesses deserve IT infrastructure that works reliably, scales without drama, and is backed by genuine technical expertise — not sales pitches.

We've worked across enterprise environments handling everything from BGP-routed campus networks to hybrid Azure deployments, and we bring that same depth of experience to every client engagement, regardless of size.

Our approach is documentation-first. Every project includes complete infrastructure documentation, knowledge transfer to your team, and monitoring configured before we walk out the door. You should understand your own environment — that's non-negotiable for us.

Based in Northeast Ohio, we work with municipalities, healthcare organizations, and growing businesses that need infrastructure they can trust.
