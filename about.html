About

A small IT company in Rittman, Ohio

Packet Point Technologies does network engineering and IT support for municipalities, nonprofits, and growing businesses around Northeast Ohio.

Why we started

IT you can actually understand

We started Packet Point because too many organizations end up with IT they don’t understand: a network installed by a vendor who has since moved on, no diagrams, and nobody who can say for sure what a given switch does. We wanted to be the kind of IT company we’d hire ourselves.

Our background is enterprise work: BGP-routed campus networks, hybrid Azure environments, compliance-heavy setups, and large switching projects. We bring the same care to a ten-person office that we would to a city network.

Every project ends with documentation and a handoff. You should understand your own environment, and you shouldn’t be stuck with us if you’d rather not be.

What we care about

How we try to run things

Straight answers

We tell you what you need to hear, not what’s easiest to sell. If a simpler fix solves the problem, that’s the one we recommend, even when it means less work for us.

Documentation you actually get

Network diagrams, runbooks, IP addressing, device configs. All of it, on every project. Your team should be able to work without us.

Reliability built in

Redundancy, monitoring, and failover are part of the design from the start, not upgrades we try to sell you later.

Thinking a few years out

We plan for where you’ll be in three years, not just next month, so the network doesn’t need to be ripped out the moment you grow.
