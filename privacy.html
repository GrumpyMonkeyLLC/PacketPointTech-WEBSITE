Privacy Policy

Plain English: we collect only what you send us, use it to respond to you, and never sell it.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how Packet Point Technologies LLC (“Packet Point,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you visit packetpointtechnologies.com or contact us through it.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit our contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, company, email address, the service you’re interested in, and your message. If you email or call us directly, we receive whatever you choose to share.

Information collected automatically

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and our own code does not set cookies. Like virtually every website, our hosting provider (Microsoft Azure) may process standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type in server logs to deliver and secure the site.

If you use the light/dark theme toggle, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. This stays on your device and is never sent to us.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the services you request
  • To follow up about a consultation or project you asked about
  • To protect our website and contact form from spam and abuse

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of providers to operate this site:

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form submissions to our inbox. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.
  • hCaptcha protects the contact form from bots. hCaptcha collects information such as your IP address and device and browser characteristics, and may set cookies, to determine whether you are a human. Your use of hCaptcha is subject to the hCaptcha Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Microsoft Azure hosts this website. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Our website fonts are hosted on our own servers, so loading the site does not send your information to font providers.

How long we keep information

We keep contact form submissions and related correspondence only as long as needed to respond to you and maintain normal business records, then delete them.

How we protect information

This site is served exclusively over HTTPS, and we limit access to inquiries to the people who need it to respond. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please don’t send passwords, network credentials, or other sensitive data through the contact form.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you’ve sent us at any time by emailing support@packetpointtechnologies.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor them as required.

Our website does not track visitors across sites, so it does not respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at support@packetpointtechnologies.com or (330) 347-5329.
